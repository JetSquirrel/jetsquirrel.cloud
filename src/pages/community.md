## Observability Group

As the observability UGL (User Group Leader) at AWS User Group China, I organize talks and meetups on metrics, logs, traces, and alerting. I’m also an AWS Community Builder.

## GreptimeDB Advocator

I’m part of the [GreptimeDB Advocator Program](https://greptime.com/blogs/2025-02-20-greptimedb-advocator-program), which brings together developers working on time-series databases, cloud, and open source. As an advocator, I write and share technical content about GreptimeDB and observability, and work with the Greptime team on what users run into in practice.

You can find my posts on [Medium](https://medium.com/@jetdeng) and [X / Twitter](https://x.com/JetSquirrel2048).

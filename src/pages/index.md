## Reliability &amp; Observability

I work as an SRE, keeping production systems healthy and understandable. Most of my day-to-day lives in Kubernetes, AWS, and the observability stack around them—metrics, logs, traces, and alerting.

I lead the observability track of **AWS User Group China** and am an **AWS Community Builder** and a **GreptimeDB Advocator**. I’m interested in Observability (O11y), AIOps, AI security, and cloud native infrastructure.

## DuckLocal

[DuckLocal](https://ducklocal.app) is a local-first workspace for querying and exploring your data, built natively on DuckDB. Open CSV, Parquet, JSON, or Excel files, query them with SQL, and keep everything on your own machine. It’s written in Rust with GPUI Kit, and ships a JSON CLI so AI agents can use it too.

## CloudBridge

[CloudBridge](https://cloudbridge.jetsquirrel.cloud) puts every cloud and AI bill in one place. It collects costs from AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, Cloudflare, OpenAI, Anthropic, and more, stores them in a local DuckDB file, and alerts you when spending jumps—no SaaS dashboard needed.

## KhonsuGraph

[KhonsuGraph](https://khonsugraph.com) is an open-source, local-first graph investigation workbench: Cypher and SQL, path exploration, evidence that cites its source records, and a JSON CLI for AI agents. It’s built for threat hunting, blast-radius analysis, and any question that is really about relationships.

## Native Shell

Lately I’ve been building with [Native Shell](https://nsh.dev): [nsh-o11y](https://jetsquirrel.github.io/nsh-o11y/), observability charts that stay fast at millions of points; [Petal](https://github.com/JetSquirrel/petal), a visual explorer for Neo4j, Neptune, NebulaGraph, and other graph databases; and [Pager](https://github.com/JetSquirrel/pager), an on-call pager running on a Cloudflare Worker that rings every device you own.
